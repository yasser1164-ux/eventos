01 — About

Technical credibility, built for leadership.

I'm a mechanical engineer with five years of experience spanning municipal site execution, commercial-facility maintenance, and Saudi Aramco capital projects. Since joining Worley in February 2022, I've progressed from Graduate Mechanical Engineer to Mechanical Engineer I — working across front-end field verification, multidisciplinary review, detailed-design and package review, technical procurement, and vendor data across mechanical, piping and facilities scopes.
